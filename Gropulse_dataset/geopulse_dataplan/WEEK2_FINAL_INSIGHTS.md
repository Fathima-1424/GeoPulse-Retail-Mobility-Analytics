
# GeoPulse - Week 2 Final Retail Mobility Insights

## 1. Project Objective
To analyse GPS mobility data and understand
movement patterns that can support retail
location planning and business decisions.

## 2. Geographic Insights
- Geographic analysis helps understand
  the distribution of mobility records.
- Location patterns can support retail
  area evaluation.

## 3. Device Movement Insights
- Device movement analysis helps understand
  movement distance and speed.
- Time gaps help identify intervals
  between consecutive GPS records.

## 4. Hotspot Insights
- Hotspot analysis helps identify frequently
  recorded geographic locations.
- These locations can be considered for
  further retail location evaluation.

## 5. Temporal Insights
- Hourly analysis helps identify periods
  with higher GPS activity.
- Daily analysis helps compare activity
  across weekdays.

## 6. Potential Retail Applications
- Support preliminary retail location planning.
- Understand geographic activity patterns.
- Identify potentially high-activity areas.
- Support time-based retail planning.

## 7. Limitations
- GPS records do not directly represent
  actual customer visits or purchases.
- Device activity does not necessarily
  represent unique customers.
- Retail decisions require additional
  business and demographic information.

## 8. Conclusion
GeoPulse demonstrates how GPS mobility data
can be analysed to discover geographic,
movement, hotspot, and temporal patterns.

The findings provide a starting point for
data-driven retail mobility analysis.
